# Stock and Receiving

This chapter covers the store: seeing what is on the floor, counting it, putting stickers on old stock, receiving raw material, and moving or writing off stock. Every change is recorded with your name.

Who uses this: storekeepers, gate staff, site managers, and the CEO and group admin for viewing.

### Stock (Stock > Stock)
**Who:** storekeeper, site manager, CEO, group admin, sales  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to see what is in the store now, by product and by zone, counted from the stickers still in stock.
**How to use it:**
1. Open Stock. The top figures show the number of bundles (or packs), the kilos, and the age of the last count.
2. Use the search box, or the product type tabs, to find a product in "Stock by product".
3. Select a row to open its details: pieces, kilos, standard weight, where it sits by zone, prices and the newest stickers.
4. In "Store map", press "Count this zone" under any zone to start counting it.
5. Use "Move" to go to Movements, or "Count" to go to Stock count. Shortcut: g s.
**Good to know:**
- Stock that was there before stickers existed is not shown until it is tagged (see Tagging).
- A warning appears when standard weights are still "to be measured". Treat those kilos as estimates.

### Stock count (Stock > Stock count)
**Who:** storekeeper, site manager  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to count what is physically in one zone and compare it with what the system expects. Shortcut: g n.
**How to use it:**
1. Under "Zone", choose the zone you are standing in.
2. In "Count each product", type the number you counted for every product. Enter or the down arrow moves to the next line. On a tablet, tap a line and use the number pad.
3. If you find a product that is not on the sheet, use "Add a product to this count".
4. If any number differs from the system, write the reason in "Why do the counts differ?". It needs at least three characters.
5. Press "Record count".
**Good to know:**
- You count bundles, not kilos. The gate scale checks kilos.
- Differences are booked as adjustments and flagged for the Site Manager.
- If a count is already locked for the day, a red message appears. A manager can use "Reopen this count" after giving a reason.
- "Past counts" lists earlier counts. "Clear" empties the sheet.

### Tagging (Stock > Tagging)
**Who:** storekeeper, site manager  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to give a label record to stock that was already in the store before stickers were used.
**How to use it:**
1. Choose the "Zone" where you are standing.
2. Choose the "Product". Its pieces and standard weight show underneath.
3. Press "Record label" once for each bundle you count. The sticker preview shows the new code.
4. When the zone is finished, a summary opens. Press "Next zone" to carry on, or "Keep tagging here".
5. Optional: press "Set estimate" and enter a rough total of standing stock, so the bar shows how far you have come.
**Good to know:**
- The page title on screen is "Label existing stock". Physical printing is not connected: no paper sticker comes out, so stick your own sticker if you have one.
- Tagged stock appears on the Stock page straight away and can be scanned onto a lorry.
- The estimate and the session counts are only a guide. They are not a stock count.

### Receive (Stock > Receive)
**Who:** storekeeper, gate, site manager  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** the gate screen for supplier deliveries.
**How to use it:**
1. Open Receive. At Mats and Bags, the screen explains that raw material cannot be booked here yet.
2. Press "Open Purchases" (or "Go to Purchases"), choose the order, and enter the kilos shown by the scale.
3. At Recycling, press "Open collector intake". Collector scrap is weighed and paid on that screen.
4. Press "Movements" to see what has been booked.
**Good to know:**
- The note on screen tells you how many orders are still waiting to be received.
- Finished goods are booked through Tagging and Movements, not here.

### Purchases (Stock > Purchases)
**Who:** site manager, cashier, storekeeper, CEO, group admin  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to record raw material bought locally or imported, and to see the true cost of each kilo after freight, clearing and duties. Shortcut: g y.
**How to use it:**
1. To add an order, press "New purchase" (site manager, storekeeper, CEO and group admin only).
2. Fill in "Supplier", "Material", "Quantity" in kg, "Unit price per kg" and "Currency". Use "Origin country" only for imports.
3. Press "Add cost" for each extra cost such as freight or duties.
4. Check the "Landed cost, live" figure, then press "Save purchase".
5. Select an order to open it. Press the "Mark ..." button to move it to the next stage: Ordered, In transit, At border, Received.
6. When the lorry arrives, enter "Kg received on the scale" and press "Receive" (site manager and storekeeper only).
**Good to know:**
- If less arrived than ordered, the shortfall is shown and the cost per kg is spread over the kilos that arrived.
- Materials come from Settings. If the list is empty, add them there first.
- "Export CSV" downloads the list.

### Movements (Stock > Movements)
**Who:** storekeeper, site manager  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to move, adjust or write off stock, and to read the full history of every movement.
**How to use it:**
1. In "Kind of move", choose "Between zones", "To another site", "Adjustment" or "Scrap".
2. Choose the "Product", then the "From zone". For transfers choose the "To zone" or "Destination site".
3. Enter the "Quantity". For an adjustment, choose "Add to the zone" or "Take out of the zone".
4. Write a "Reason". It is required for adjustments and scrap.
5. Press "Record movement".
6. For scrap, enter the "Label code" printed on the label you are writing off, and a reason.
7. Use the ledger below to search, filter by kind, or press "Export".
**Good to know:**
- If another site has sent you stock, "Arriving from another site" appears. Pick the zone, confirm which of your products it is, then press "Book in".
- A warning shows if you move more than the zone holds. Nothing is overwritten; every line is kept with your name.
- The server may refuse a move. Read the red message.
