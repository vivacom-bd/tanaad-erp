# Sales

This chapter covers orders, clients and invoices. Local clients pay cash before delivery. Export clients buy on credit that the CEO approves.

Who uses this: sales, cashiers, storekeepers, site managers, and the CEO.

### Orders (Sales > Orders)
**Who:** sales, site manager, cashier, storekeeper, CEO, group admin  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to create orders and follow each one from cash or approval to loading, invoice and payment. Shortcut: g o.
**How to use it:**
1. Press "New order".
2. Step 1, "Client": search and choose the client. The terms and, for export clients, the credit used are shown.
3. Step 2, "Line items": choose a product and enter the bundles. Add more lines as needed. Remove a line with the bin button.
4. Step 3, "Check and create": check the bundles, the expected cargo weight and the total. Press "Create order".
5. To follow an order, click its tab or the pipeline stage: "Awaiting cash", "Awaiting approval", "To load", "To invoice", "Unpaid", "Paid" or "Held". Click an order to open it.
6. In the order, the main button depends on the stage: "Record cash received", "Open credit desk" (CEO), "Open dispatch", "Issue invoice" or "Record payment".
**Good to know:**
- A local order stays "Awaiting cash" until the cashier confirms the cash. The store stays locked until then.
- An export order goes to the CEO credit desk. A warning shows if it takes the client over their limit.
- A line priced below the list needs approval before dispatch.
- Prices are set by the server. Figures on screen are an estimate until the order saves.

### Clients (Sales > Clients)
**Who:** sales, site manager, CEO, group admin  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to see every client's balance, credit and orders, and to add new clients.
**How to use it:**
1. Use the tabs "All", "Local - cash" and "Export - credit", or the search box.
2. Click a client to open the profile: credit, shipment history, invoices and payments.
3. In the profile, press "New order" to start an order for this client, or "Send statement on WhatsApp" to see a statement preview.
4. To add a client, press "New client".
5. Fill in "Business name", "WhatsApp number" and the other details. Choose the "Channel", "Country" and "Invoice currency".
6. For export clients, set "Credit limit" and "Credit days". Press "Save client".
**Good to know:**
- Only the CEO and group admin can set credit terms. Others can add local clients only.
- Orders above the credit limit go to the CEO.
- The WhatsApp share is a preview. Nothing is sent.

### Invoices (Sales > Invoices)
**Who:** sales, cashier, site manager, CEO  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to review invoices, client statements and recorded payments. Shortcut: g i.
**How to use it:**
1. Choose a tab: "Invoices", "Statements" or "Payments".
2. On "Invoices", filter by "All", "Unpaid", "Overdue" or "Paid", or search by invoice or client.
3. Click an invoice to see it. Press "Print / PDF" to print or save it.
4. Press "Share on WhatsApp" to see the message preview.
5. Press the order button to go to the order behind the invoice.
6. Press "Export" to download the list.
**Good to know:**
- Invoices are issued from Orders, after the lorry has passed the gate. Record payments there too.
- The page warns that EFRIS is not connected. Invoices are not yet fiscal invoices.
- Sharing is only a preview. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp.
