# Group Portal

The Group portal shows all three sites together. The CEO and the group admin use it to see how the company is doing and to make decisions.

Who uses this: the CEO and the group admin. (The CEO's main pages are Overview, Approvals, Credit and Morning brief. The group admin's are Overview, Settings, People and Audit log.)

### Overview (Overview)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g c.
**What it is for:** the state of every site on one screen.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Overview**.
2. Read the top tiles: **Cash**, **Open exceptions**, **Awaiting approval** and **Receivables**. Click a tile to see the detail per site.
3. Choose **Cards** or **Matrix** (key V) to see sites side by side or as a comparison table.
4. On a site card press **Open portal** (keys 1, 2, 3) to go to that site.
5. Press **Approvals** to go to decisions. Use **Refresh** to reload when you see it.
**Good to know:**
- Further down: cash in and out per day, **Export receivables**, **Money out today**, **Alerts**, **Open items across sites** and **Latest on the record**.
- A dash instead of a number means the figure is not available, with the reason on hover.

### Approvals (Decisions > Approvals)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g a.
**What it is for:** to decide every open exception across the sites, one at a time.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Approvals**. Use **Open** or **Decided today**. Narrow the list with **Search exceptions**, the site buttons and the kind buttons.
2. Click an exception. Read the numbers and records shown.
3. Press **Approve** (A), **Hold** (H) or **Resolve** (R).
4. Type a **Reason**, then press **Confirm** (Ctrl+Enter). Press Esc to go back.
5. To report a problem yourself, press **Raise exception** and fill Site, Kind, Severity, Title and Detail.
**Good to know:**
- A hold needs a note in the connected system.
- Your name and reason go on the audit log.
- Deciding closes the exception on the record. Gate unlocks, stock and cash still happen on the site screens.
- Export orders waiting for a decision appear in **Orders waiting for approval** with Approve and Hold.

### Credit (Decisions > Credit)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g r.
**What it is for:** to approve export orders and watch what clients owe.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Credit**. The tiles show **To approve**, **Total receivables**, **Overdue**, **Top exposure** and **Credit lines used**.
2. In **Approval queue**, click an order. Read its lines and **Credit picture**, which shows credit used after the order.
3. Press **Approve** (A) or **Hold** (H), type a reason, then **Confirm**.
4. In **Export receivables**, search clients and filter **All**, **With a balance** or **Past terms**.
5. Press **Preview statement**, then **Copy** to paste it into WhatsApp. Press **Export** (E) for a CSV.
**Good to know:**
- Local orders never need approval. They are cash before delivery.
- A hold needs a reason. Sales sees it on the order.
- Approving releases the order to the store for picking.

### Morning brief (Records > Morning brief)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g b.
**What it is for:** a short daily message with five numbers per site.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Morning brief** and read the **Preview**.
2. Press **Copy message** (Ctrl+Enter).
3. Paste it into WhatsApp yourself.
4. If the button is shown, press **Mark sent on WhatsApp** to log it.
**Good to know:**
- The page says "Daily auto-send is not wired yet." You send it by hand.
- **Recipients** lists who gets it. **Alert limits** shows the thresholds that raise alerts.

### Audit log (Records > Audit log)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g u.
**What it is for:** a permanent record of who changed what, where and why.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Audit log**. Tiles count edits, reopenings and overrides.
2. Use **Search the feed**, the site buttons and the type buttons (**All**, **Edits and reopenings**, Edit, Reopening, Override, Variance, Normal).
3. Read the **Timeline**, newest first. Click a line to open **Audit line**.
4. Press **Export** (E) for a CSV.
**Good to know:**
- It is append-only. Lines cannot be edited or deleted. A correction adds a new line.
- **Offline queue** shows entries still waiting to reach the server.

### People (Finance > People)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g p.
**What it is for:** the workforce across all sites: numbers, wage bill and contracts.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **People**. Read the tiles: **Workforce**, **On shift now**, **Weekly wage bill**, **Shared workers**, **To renew**, **New joiners**.
2. Check **Contracts ending**. Press **Renew** on a row, or **Renew next** (R) for the most urgent.
3. Choose a contract term and press **Renew for ... days**.
4. Press **Export CSV** (E) to download.
**Good to know:**
- Renewing is limited to site managers and group admins. For others the button is off.
- Wage figures are estimates from active rates.
- Shared workers' costs follow the days worked at each site.

### P&L (Finance > P&L)
**Who:** CEO, group admin  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g l.
**What it is for:** sales, costs and profit by site, and the cost of making each product.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **P&L**. Choose the period (**Today**, **3 days** or **7 days**) or, on the connected system, step by month with the arrows.
2. Read the tiles and the **P&L statement**.
3. Check **Unit cost per product** and filter by site.
4. Press **Print** (P) or **Export CSV** (E).
5. On the connected system, a manager, CEO or group admin can press **Close period**, or **Reopen** with a reason.
**Good to know:**
- Figures from days not yet closed are marked **Provisional**.
- The expense categories are fixed at entry, so nothing is uncategorised.

### Settings (Settings)
**Who:** CEO and group admin can open it; only a group admin can change group-wide lists  **Site:** all. Shortcut: g ,.
**What it is for:** the same settings screen as at a site, with all sites open.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Settings** and choose a site with the **Site** buttons.
2. Pick a section on the left. See chapter "Reports and Settings" for each one.
**Good to know:**
- The CEO can view but not change settings.
