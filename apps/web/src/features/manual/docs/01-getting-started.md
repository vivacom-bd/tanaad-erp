# Getting Started

This chapter shows you how to sign in, choose where to work, and find your way around the screen. It also covers keyboard shortcuts and what to do when the connection drops.

Who uses this: everyone.

## Signing in

Open the app and you see the **Sign in** screen. There are two ways in.

### Office account (Sign in with username and password)
**Who:** office staff, managers, the CEO and group admins  **Site:** all
**What it is for:** signing in from a computer or your own device.
**How to use it:**
1. Stay on the **Office account** tab.
2. Type your **Username**.
3. Type your **Password**. Tap the eye icon to show or hide it.
4. Press **Sign in**.
**Good to know:**
- If you leave a box empty you see "Enter your username." or "Enter your password."
- Your username and password are given to you by your manager. Keep them private.

### Shared tablet (Site and 4-digit PIN)
**Who:** floor staff such as supervisors, storekeepers, gate staff, mechanics and cashiers  **Site:** the site the tablet belongs to
**What it is for:** quick sign-in on a tablet that many people share.
**How to use it:**
1. Open the **Shared tablet** tab.
2. Under **This tablet belongs to**, choose your site.
3. Under **Who are you?**, tap your name.
4. Enter your 4-digit PIN on the number pad ("Enter your 4-digit PIN").
**Good to know:**
- A wrong PIN shows "Wrong PIN. Try again." and you can try again.
- Sign out when you finish so the next person starts clean.

## The Portals screen

After signing in you see **Choose where to work**. Each card is a workspace you are allowed to use. Only your own workspaces appear.

1. Look at the cards. "All sites" is the group overview. The others are Mats, Bags and Recycling.
2. Each card shows today's **Attendance records** and **Open exceptions**.
3. If you have more than one role at a site, choose one under **Open as**.
4. Press **Open workspace**.

Use the sun/moon button for light or dark theme. Use **Sign out** to leave. To come back to this screen later, open your name menu at the top right and choose **Switch Portal / Site** (shortcut: Alt+P).

## The screen layout

- **Menu (left side):** the pages you may use, grouped by area such as Production, Stock or Cash. You only see pages your role allows. Press Ctrl+B to collapse or expand it. On a phone the menu opens from the menu button, and a bottom bar shows your four main pages plus **More**.
- **Top bar:** shows where you are, a **Search** box, an **Alerts** bell, a full-screen button, a theme button and your name menu (**Account**).
- **Work area tabs:** pages that belong together (for example Machine wall, Attendance and Shift handover) show as tabs under the top bar. Press "," for the previous tab and "." for the next.
- **Bottom status bar (large screens):** shows the company, the current shift, today's date and the cash day status.
- **Your name menu:** Switch Portal / Site, theme, Touch layout or Compact layout, Keyboard shortcuts, Install as an app (when offered) and Sign Out. Use **Touch layout** on tablets for bigger buttons.

### Today page
**Who:** all site roles  **Site:** the site you opened
**What it is for:** your starting point each shift, with a short list of tasks for your role.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Today** (Shortcut: g t).
2. Read your tasks and any counts in the orange badges.
3. Press **Open** on a task to go to that page.

### Site overview
**Who:** all site roles (managers land here first)  **Site:** the site you opened
**What it is for:** the whole site at a glance.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Site overview** (Shortcut: g d).
2. Click any tile to jump to the page behind it.
See chapter "Site pages" for details.

## Search and the command palette

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), or click **Search pages and records…**, to open the command palette.

1. Type a page name, a worker, an order number, a client, a product, a machine or a label code.
2. Move with the up and down arrows.
3. Press Enter to open the result. Press Esc to close.

The palette also switches theme, switches layout, opens the shortcut list, lets you work offline or reconnect, switches portal and signs you out.

## When you are offline

If the connection is lost, a red **Offline** badge appears at the top and the bottom bar turns red. Keep working. New entries are saved on the device and the badge shows how many are **queued**. When the connection returns, they are sent.

If the server refuses an entry, a cloud icon (**Sync problems**) appears with a number. Press it to open **Not accepted by the server**. For each item you see what it was and why it was refused. Choose **Dismiss** to clear it, or **Retry** (when offered) to send it again. A small "queued" count means items are still waiting to send.

## Who sees which menu items

"All sites" means every site. Some pages exist only at one site (for example Mixer at Mats). Today, Site overview and Scan label show for every role.

| Role | Pages in the menu |
|---|---|
| Supervisor | Machine wall, site production pages, Shift handover, Print labels, Attendance, Casual sign-up, Workers, Breakdowns |
| Cashier | Cash book, Record expense, Wages, Purchases, Orders, Invoices, Scrap weigh-in and Collector ledger (Recycling) |
| Storekeeper | Stock, Stock count, Tagging, Receive, Purchases, Movements, Load lorry, Deliveries, Spares, Orders, Print labels, Scrap weigh-in (Recycling) |
| Gate | Receive, Load lorry, Scrap weigh-in (Recycling) |
| Mechanic | Machine wall, Spares, Breakdowns |
| Sales | Stock, Deliveries, Orders, Clients, Invoices |
| Site manager | Everything at the site, including Reports and Settings |
| Driver | Deliveries |
| CEO | Machine wall, Shift handover, Attendance, Workers, Wages, Cash book, Stock, Purchases, Load lorry, Deliveries, Spares, Breakdowns, Orders, Clients, Invoices, Collector ledger (Recycling), Reports, Settings; plus the Group portal |
| Group admin | Machine wall, Shift handover, Attendance, Workers, Wages, Cash book, Stock, Purchases, Load lorry, Spares, Orders, Clients, Reports, Settings; plus the Group portal |

If you open a page your role does not include, you see "You don't have access to this page" with buttons to go to your home page or **All portals**. Ask your site manager if you need access.
