# Maintenance

This chapter covers the spare parts store and machine breakdowns. Parts and downtime are charged to the machine.

Who uses this: mechanics, storekeepers, supervisors, site managers, and the CEO.

### Spares (Maintenance > Spares)
**Who:** mechanic, storekeeper, site manager, CEO, group admin  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to issue spare parts against a machine and a mechanic, and to book new parts into the locked store. Shortcut: g r.
**How to use it:**
1. To take a part out, press "Issue part". Only the storekeeper and supervisor see this button.
2. Fill in "1 - Part", "2 - Quantity", "3 - Machine" and "4 - Mechanic".
3. Confirm. Depending on the setup, you press "Issue" under your own sign-in, or enter the PIN.
4. A summary shows the cost booked to the machine and what is left. Press "Issue another part" to continue.
5. To add stock, press "Receive parts", choose the "Part", enter the quantity and press "Book in".
6. Use the tabs "Parts", "Issues log" and "Low stock and reorder" to check levels.
**Good to know:**
- You cannot issue more than is on hand. A warning shows if the issue takes the part below its minimum.
- The "Low stock and reorder" tab shows how short each part is and the quantity to order.
- Cost per machine is shown on the right.

### Breakdowns (Maintenance > Breakdowns)
**Who:** mechanic, site manager, supervisor, CEO  **Site:** all sites
**What it is for:** to report a machine that has stopped and record when it runs again. Shortcut: g b.
**How to use it:**
1. Press "Report breakdown" the minute a machine stops.
2. Choose the "Machine".
3. Choose the cause from the list. If none fits, type it in "Something else".
4. Press "Report ... down". The downtime clock starts.
5. When it is repaired, press "Resolve - back in production" under "Down now".
6. Use the "Breakdown ledger" to search past breakdowns. Filter by "Down now" or "Resolved". Select a row to see its spares cost and downtime.
**Good to know:**
- The top figures show machines down now, downtime for the last 7 days, spares cost and average repair time.
- Spares issued against the machine are added to its breakdown cost.
- The list of causes is kept in Settings.
