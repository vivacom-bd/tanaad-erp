# Site Pages: Today and Site Overview

These two pages are where you start at a site. Today tells you what to do. Site overview shows how the site is doing.

Who uses this: everyone at a site (Today); managers, the CEO and group admins most (Site overview).

### Today (Today)
**Who:** all site roles  **Site:** the site you opened. Shortcut: g t.
**What it is for:** a short list of the jobs for your role in the current shift.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Today**. The top shows your site, your role, the shift (Day or Night with its hours) and the date.
2. Read **Your tasks**. Each task has a short line of detail. An orange number means something needs attention.
3. Press **Open** next to a task to go to that page.
4. Read **Open exceptions** below. Each has a severity label (critical, warning or info) and the role that owns it.
**What you see by role:**
- Supervisor: Production entries, Attendance, Shift handover log. At Recycling also Collector intake.
- Site manager: Site overview, Production entries, Attendance, Cash day.
- Cashier: Cash day, Record an expense. At Recycling also Collector ledger.
- Storekeeper: Stock overview, Receive goods, Dispatch.
- Gate: Receive at gate, Dispatch and gate pass, Label lookup.
- Mechanic: Breakdowns, Spares store, the machine wall.
- Sales: Customer orders, Clients and credit, Invoices.
- Driver: Deliveries.
- Any other role: Production entries and Attendance.
**Good to know:**
- Site managers, the CEO and group admins see every open exception at the site. Other roles see only the ones owned by their role.
- "No open exceptions assigned to this role" means you have nothing waiting.

### Site overview (Site overview)
**Who:** all site roles can open it. Site managers, the CEO and group admins land here first.  **Site:** the site you opened. Shortcut: g d.
**What it is for:** the whole site at a glance: people, output, cash, stock and problems.
**How to use it:**
1. Open **Site overview**. A strip of tiles shows **Headcount today**, **Waste, measured** and **Cash expected**. The fourth tile is **Intake today** at Recycling, or **Stock on hand** at the other sites.
2. Click a tile to open the page behind it.
3. In the machine panel, switch between **Map** and **Table**. Each machine shows On target, Below target, Not logged, Down or Arriving. Click one to open the machine wall. Use **Shift sheet** to open the handover.
4. Read **Needs a decision** for open exceptions, and **Mass balance** for kilos in, output, work in progress, waste and allowed loss.
5. Scroll down for **Output by product**, **People** (permanent, fixed-term contract, casual), **On the record** (latest changes), **Maintenance**, **Dispatches today**, and **Export receivables** (or **Yield per line** at Recycling).
**Good to know:**
- Mass balance says Balanced, Within allowed loss, or shows how many kilos are unaccounted for.
- Maintenance lists machines that are down and spare parts below their minimum, marked **Reorder**.
- Some sites show an orange note about details still to be confirmed.
