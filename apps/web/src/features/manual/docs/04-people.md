# People

The People pages cover who works at the site, who came in today, and how workers are paid. Attendance feeds the wage runs, so marking it correctly matters.
Who uses this: supervisors, site managers and cashiers, with the CEO and group admin viewing or approving.

### Attendance (People > Attendance)
**Who:** Supervisor, Site manager, CEO, Group admin  **Site:** All sites. Shortcut: g a.
**What it is for:** The digital attendance sheet: the last six working days across, workers down, with today editable.
**How to use it:**
1. Choose **Day** or **Night** shift at the top. Use the sector chips to narrow the list, or the search box to find a worker.
2. In the **Today · Mark Status** column, press **Present**, **Half** or **Absent** for each worker.
3. Or press **Guided Roll Call** and go through workers one at a time with **Present**, **Half day** and **Absent** (keys P, H, A).
4. Press **Mark ... unmarked Present** to mark everyone left as present in one go.
5. For extra hours, press the clock button on a worker's row, enter the **Hours beyond the shift**, and save.
**Good to know:**
- Past days are locked. Ask the Site manager to correct them.
- A worker whose contract has ended cannot be marked until you press **Renew**.
- When the site is connected to the server, changing a mark that is already set asks for a short reason, then **Save correction**. Only the Supervisor, Site manager and Group admin can mark.
- The **Archived Workers** tab lists people taken off the roster, with a **Reactivate** button.

### Casual sign-up (People > Casual sign-up)
**Who:** Supervisor, Site manager  **Site:** All sites. Shortcut: g u.
**What it is for:** Registers a new casual worker.
**How to use it:**
1. Under **ID card**, press **Take photo**.
2. Under **Face**, press **Take photo**.
3. Enter the **Full name** and the **ID number** from the card.
4. Enter the **Mobile money number**: 10 digits.
5. Choose the **Sector**.
6. Pick a usual **Daily rate** or type one.
7. Press **Register worker**.
8. Afterwards you can press **Mark present now**, **Print QR badge** or **Sign up another**.
**Good to know:**
- The camera is simulated. No image is saved.
- One phone number belongs to one worker. If it is already used, the page shows who has it and will not continue.
- The Register button stays off until every step shows done. The footer lists what is missing.
- Casuals are paid weekly with the casual wage run.

### Workers (People > Workers)
**Who:** Site manager, Supervisor, CEO, Group admin  **Site:** All sites.
**What it is for:** The full list of people who work at the site, how they are engaged and what they are paid.
**How to use it:**
1. Search by name, number, ID or phone. Narrow with the engagement tabs, the **Sector** list and the **Status** list.
2. Click a worker to open their profile: engagement, last 14 days of attendance, cash paid to them, and history.
3. For a contract worker, choose a **Renewal term** and press **Renew**.
4. Press **Archive worker** to take someone off the roster. Press **Reactivate** to bring them back.
5. Press **Sign up casual** to register a new casual, or **Export** to download the list.
**Good to know:**
- Archiving keeps the record and history.
- **End engagement** asks for a reason and is available when the site is connected to the server, to the Site manager and Group admin.
- Shared workers show "from" their home site and can only be archived there.

### Wages (People > Wages)
**Who:** Site manager, Cashier, CEO, Group admin  **Site:** All sites. Shortcut: g l. The page is titled "Settlement".
**What it is for:** Pays workers for a period. Each run works out gross pay, overtime, advances taken and the net amount for each worker.
**How to use it:**
1. Choose a tab: **Weekly casuals**, **End-of-term contracts** or **Monthly**.
2. To start a run, press **New run**. Check **From** and **To**, add any **Bonuses**, then **Create run**.
3. Someone else, not the person who created the run, presses **Approve run**.
4. Once approved, the cashier or manager presses **Pay out** on each line. Choose **Paid by** (Cash, Mobile money or Bank), add a **Reference** if you have one, and press **Confirm**.
5. To give an advance, press **Give advance**, choose the worker, enter the **Amount (UGX)** and the **Reason**.
**Good to know:**
- Approved lines are fixed. Later attendance changes do not alter them.
- A cash payout goes into today's cash book, so the day must be open.
- Press **Cancel run** to cancel a run, when the button is shown. Advances it deducted are given back.
- If a command is waiting to sync, the page shows "Waiting to sync". Each line has a **Payslip** button.
